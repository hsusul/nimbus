# Design-research asset policy

No third-party screenshots were saved during the 2026-08-01 research pass.

The reviewed public pages did not provide sufficiently clear permission to redistribute their captures in this repository, and several requested sources were blocked, redirected, empty, paid, or required manual license verification. Source URLs and paraphrased notes are therefore the durable research artifacts.

If a future researcher adds an image here:

1. Use normal public browser access; do not bypass authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, robots rules, or rate limits.
2. Verify that saving a small reference capture is allowed by the source's terms and the specific asset license.
3. Crop to the smallest area needed to explain the pattern.
4. Exclude personal data, private accounts, tokens, emails, payment information, and unrelated copyrighted material.
5. Use a descriptive filename such as `refero-dashboard-sidebar-example.png`.
6. Add an adjacent Markdown record with creator, design name, direct source URL, capture date, purpose, and rights status.
7. Never import the image into production UI or present it as Nimbus-owned work.

Existing product screenshots in `docs/assets/` are Nimbus-owned deterministic captures generated from synthetic demo data; they are not part of this third-party reference directory.
